## 🚀 Overview
Auto Video Editor for Windows takes raw recordings and turns them into a tidy first cut without hours of manual trimming. As an AI auto video editor, it detects and removes silent gaps and long pauses, cuts footage at natural break points, and arranges the remaining clips into a coherent sequence you can refine or export straight away. It is built for creators, educators, and professionals who record a lot and want the tedious editing handled automatically so they can focus on the story.

## ✨ Features
- **AI auto-cut** — automatically detects natural break points in your footage and cuts clips so you skip the manual scrubbing.
- **Silence and pause removal** — finds and trims quiet gaps, dead air, and long pauses to keep your edit tight.
- **Automatic clip arrangement** — orders and sequences your clips into a coherent timeline ready for review.
- **Fast automatic assembly** — stitches everything into a finished edit quickly, so a rough cut is ready in minutes.
- **Timeline refinement** — adjust any of the AI's decisions by hand when you want the final say over a cut.
